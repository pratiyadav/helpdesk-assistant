# Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

All Acme accounts require MFA. Approved methods are the Authenticator app and hardware security keys. SMS codes are not allowed for any account.

## Moving MFA to a new phone
Before wiping or replacing your phone, open the Self-Service Portal, choose "Security", then "Move authenticator". If you already lost access to the old phone, call the IT Service Desk on extension 5000 and verify your identity with your manager. Identity checks take about 15 minutes.

## Lost or stolen security key
Report a lost hardware key to the Service Desk within 2 hours. A temporary key is issued from the front desk on the same business day.
