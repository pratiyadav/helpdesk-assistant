# IT Service Desk Hours and Response Times

The Service Desk is staffed Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM IST. Outside those hours, only Priority 1 issues are handled through the on-call line.

Priority 1 means a complete outage or a security incident, with a response within 30 minutes. Priority 2 means work is blocked for one person, with a response within 4 business hours. Priority 3 covers issues such as how-to questions, with a response within 2 business days.

Contact the Service Desk on extension 5000 or at helpdesk@acme.example.
