# Shared Drives and Permissions

Each department has a shared drive mapped as the S: drive. Access is granted by the drive owner, who is usually the department head. Request access through the Self-Service Portal under "File access". Requests are approved or rejected within 2 business days.

Permissions are reviewed every quarter and unused access is removed after 90 days. Never share a drive folder link with external parties. Use a guest link from OneDrive with an expiry date instead.
