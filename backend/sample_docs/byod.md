# Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

Personal phones and tablets may access email and Teams after enrolling in Intune through the Company Portal. Enrollment installs a work profile and does not give IT access to personal photos, messages or browsing history.

Personal laptops are not permitted to connect to internal systems. Enrolled devices must run an operating system version released in the last 24 months, use a screen lock of 6 digits or more, and have storage encryption enabled. IT can remotely wipe only the work profile, never the whole device. Enrolled devices can use the ACME-Secure Wi-Fi network.
