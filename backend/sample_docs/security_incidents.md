# Security Incident Reporting

Report any suspected security incident to security@acme.example or call extension 5999, which is staffed 24 hours a day. A suspected incident includes malware warnings, accidental data sharing, lost devices and unexpected MFA prompts.

If your laptop may be compromised, disconnect it from the network but do not shut it down. Do not try to delete or clean anything yourself. Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 1 hour of discovery.
