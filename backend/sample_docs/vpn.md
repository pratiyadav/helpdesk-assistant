# VPN Guide

## Resetting your VPN token
If your VPN token has expired or you got a new phone, open the Self-Service Portal, choose "Security",
and click "Reset VPN token". Scan the new QR code with your authenticator app. The old token stops
working immediately.

## Error codes
VPN-403 means your account is not in the remote-access group. Ask your manager to approve access, then
wait 30 minutes for the change to sync. VPN-504 means the gateway timed out; retry on a different network.
