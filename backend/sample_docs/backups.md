# Backups and File Recovery

Laptops are not backed up. Save your work to OneDrive or the shared drive.

OneDrive keeps deleted files in the recycle bin for 93 days. The shared drive is backed up nightly at 2 AM and those backups are kept for 30 days. To restore a file that is older than the recycle bin allows, raise a ticket with the full file path and the date you need. Restores take up to 1 business day.

Databases and servers are backed up by the infrastructure team, not by individual users.
