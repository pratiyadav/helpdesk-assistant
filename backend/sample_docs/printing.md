# Printing

Printers are installed automatically on Windows laptops when you sign in at the office, so no manual setup is needed. Color printing is restricted to the second-floor printer.

Print jobs are released by tapping your access card at any printer, and unreleased jobs are deleted after 24 hours. If a printer shows a paper jam that you cannot clear, do not open the rear panel. Raise a ticket with the printer name from its label.
