# Email and Calendar

Acme uses Microsoft 365 for email. The mailbox size limit is 50 GB per user. Attachments larger than 25 MB are blocked, so share large files through the shared drive instead.

## Phishing
Report suspicious emails with the Report Phish button in Outlook. Never forward a suspicious email to colleagues.

## Shared mailboxes
Request a shared mailbox through the Self-Service Portal under "Email". Approval from the mailbox owner is required, and setup takes up to 2 business days.
