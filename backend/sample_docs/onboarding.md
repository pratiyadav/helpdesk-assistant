# New Employee IT Onboarding

IT prepares a laptop and accounts before your first day. On day one, collect your laptop from the front desk between 9:00 and 10:00 AM. Your username follows the pattern firstname.lastname. Sign in with the temporary password in your welcome email and set a new password right away.

Complete the mandatory security awareness training within 7 days of joining. Managers must submit the New Joiner form at least 5 business days before the start date, otherwise equipment may be delayed.
